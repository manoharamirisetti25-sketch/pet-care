PAW-SITIVE HABITS

Small habits, big happy tails.

Everyday care makes a lasting difference. Keep these simple tips in mind.

  • 💧
    Fresh water alwaysKeep clean drinking water available throughout the day.
  • 🥕
    Balanced nutritionChoose age-appropriate food and ask your vet about portions.
  • 🎾
    Play and exerciseMake time for walks, play and activities your pet enjoys.
  • 🩹
    Regular checkupsSchedule preventive visits and keep vaccinations up to date.
